Scenario

Context: A mid-sized tech company, PulseWave Solutions, is rolling out a new scheduling software called TimeSync. This message is to inform key stakeholders of the upcoming rollout.

Message 1: Internal Employee Communication (Staff)

Subject: Upcoming Launch of Our New Scheduling Tool: TimeSync

Hi Team,

We’re excited to announce the rollout of TimeSync, our new internal scheduling system designed to simplify calendar management and boost team collaboration.

Starting May 1, TimeSync will replace Outlook Calendars company-wide. Training sessions will begin next week, and your managers will share details soon. The system is intuitive, mobile-friendly, and integrates directly with our other tools.

Thanks in advance for your flexibility as we transition—this change is all about making your day-to-day work easier.

With appreciation,
Sasha Kim
Director of Operations

Message 2: External Customer Communication

Subject: Exciting News: A Smarter Scheduling Experience with TimeSync

Dear Valued Customer,

We’re always working to improve your experience, and we’re excited to introduce TimeSync—our new internal scheduling system launching May 1.

What does this mean for you?
You may notice faster response times and smoother coordination as our team schedules meetings and manages appointments more efficiently. While there’s no action required on your part, we want to keep you informed of the tools helping us serve you better.

As always, thank you for choosing PulseWave Solutions.

Warm regards,
Customer Experience Team
PulseWave Solutions

Message 3: Executive Leadership Communication

Subject: Strategic Rollout: TimeSync Scheduling Platform Launch

Dear Leadership Team,

As part of our ongoing commitment to operational excellence, we’re launching TimeSync, a modernized scheduling platform that will replace Outlook Calendars company-wide starting May 1.

This upgrade aligns with our Q2 digital transformation goals and is expected to improve internal collaboration, reduce scheduling conflicts by 40%, and streamline workflow across departments.

Your support will be critical in reinforcing adoption during team meetings. Managers will receive rollout guidelines and timelines later this week.

Please reach out if you have feedback or suggestions as we implement this change.

Sincerely,
Sasha Kim
Director of Operations
